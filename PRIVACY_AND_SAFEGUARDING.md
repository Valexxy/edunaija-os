# LearnNaija — Privacy & Safeguarding (NDPA 2023)

## 1. Regulatory Compliance
LearnNaija complies with the **Nigeria Data Protection Act (NDPA 2023)** and international standards for minors:
- **Data Minimization:** We collect only what is essential for educational diagnostic personalization.
- **Auditable Parental Consent:** Minors cannot create public social profiles. Guardian links require explicit parental consent timestamps (`consent_recorded_at`).
- **No Manipulative Advertising:** Children's learning history, test scores, and tutor conversations are never sold or used for targeted commercial advertising.
- **Account Deletion & Data Portability:** Families can export or delete their child's learning history at any time.
